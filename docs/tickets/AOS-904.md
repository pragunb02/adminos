## Description
The owner sets weekly or monthly limits on a habit lens, a merchant or a category, and gets one push at 50%, 80% and 100% of each limit, once per threshold per period. Owner's changes (2026-10-17):
1. **Learning month:** no limits for the first 30 days of data. The leaks page shows "Learning your habits: N of 30 days".
2. **After 30 days, suggested limits** come from the owner's own trend, with the rule and its numbers under "How we got this". The owner accepts, adjusts or dismisses each. **Nothing pushes for a limit the owner hasn't accepted.** Every limit stays editable.
3. Payments to people by UPI never count (D-81).

## Why
D-80: the coach must reach the owner before the month is gone, with few nudges. BRD §5.6.

## Scope
- **In:** limits (CRUD API + Settings UI), limit status on the leaks page, learning state, suggestions (accept / adjust / not now), the alert check (on each new transaction and every 10 minutes), a sent-record ledger, a "limit alerts" switch, push tap → `/leaks`.
- **Out:** the evening digest (AOS-905) and the report card (AOS-906), which read the same statuses.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A limit has a scope (lens, merchant or category), a period (weekly Mon–Sun or monthly, in the user's zone) and a positive amount; one limit per scope and period.
- [x] Crossing 50%, 80% or 100% pushes once per threshold per period, across restarts and instances; several thresholds crossed at once give one push (the highest).
- [x] A refund that drops spending below a threshold does not re-arm it in the same period; a new period starts fresh.
- [x] Creating or editing a limit records thresholds already passed in the current period silently (no instant nag).
- [x] During the first 30 days of data no limit can be created (409), and the status says "N of 30 days".
- [x] After 30 days each lens with a habit gets a suggestion with its rule and numbers; accepting creates the limit; "Not now" hides it for 30 days; lenses with a limit get none.
- [x] The alert switch off means no limit pushes; other users' limits are 404.
- [x] Settings lists, adds, edits and deletes limits; the leaks page shows each lens's limit status.

## Design
- **Context:** leaks (AOS-902) give per-lens facts; the dispatcher (D-51) sends through channels; the scheduler (D-50) claims slots, but alerts are per (limit, period, threshold), finer than a job slot.
- **Options considered (alert idempotency):**
  1. **Flags on the limit row** (`sent50`, `sent80`…) reset at period start: a reset job is a second moving part, and a missed reset loses a period.
  2. **A ledger row per (limit, period start, threshold)** with a primary key (chosen): `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` is the claim; it can't double-send across instances or restarts, and a new period is just a new key.
  3. **Look for an earlier notification** with the same data: notifications are per channel and may be pruned; fragile.
- **Options considered (when to check):** a `TransactionCreatedListener` alone (misses re-categorisations and lens edits), a job alone (up to 10 min late), **both** (chosen: immediate for new payments, the job catches everything else; the ledger makes the double check harmless).
- **Decision:** ledger + listener + 10-minute job. **Patterns:** Strategy for scopes (`LimitScope` sealed: Lens / Merchant / Category, each knows which facts it counts), Repository (limits, ledger, dismissals, settings; in-memory + Exposed), a pure `LimitMath` for status and thresholds, a pure `LimitSuggester` for suggestions.
  - **Claim, then send:** the ledger row is written before the push. A crash between the two loses that one push (at most once) rather than repeating it; repeated nudges are what makes people ignore them (D-80).
  - **Refunds don't re-arm** within the period: the ledger keeps the threshold claimed; the status shown is still exact (spent may fall back below).
  - **Learning month:** days of data = days from the first spend or refund (local day) to today, inclusive; ready at 30.
  - **Suggestion rule:** per lens, weekly limit = median of the last 4 complete Monday–Sunday weeks of that lens's spending, minus 10%, rounded down to a multiple of ₹50 (at least ₹100). Only when the lens had spending in at least 2 of those weeks, it has no limit yet, and it wasn't dismissed in the last 30 days. Shown with the four weekly amounts, the median, the step and the rounding. A weekly limit gives feedback inside a week (faster than monthly), and the median ignores one unusual week.
  - **Scope counting:** lens = the lens's facts (person-to-person excluded, D-81); category = that category's spend and refunds, person-to-person excluded; merchant = that merchant exactly (the owner named it, so it counts even if it looks like a person).
  - **Quiet hours** apply as for every non-critical push (D-51): a 23:30 crossing arrives at the end of quiet hours.
- **Extensibility:** a new scope is one `LimitScope` case; a new threshold set is one constant; another suggestion rule is one `LimitSuggester` implementation.
- **Impact:** `domain/coach/limits/*`, coach wiring, `notification_type` gains `coach` (`NotificationType.COACH`, always enabled at the notification level; the coach's own switch gates it), Web Push tap URL for `entity_type=coach` → `/leaks`, `V35__coach_limits.sql`; web Settings "Limits" section, leaks page banner/status/suggestions.

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** authenticated routes `GET/POST /coach/limits`, `PATCH/DELETE /coach/limits/{id}`, `POST /coach/limits/suggestions/{lens}/dismiss`, `GET/PATCH /coach/settings`; four tables; a listener and a 10-minute job; pushes whose text contains limit labels and amounts.
- **Abuse and what stops it:**
  - *Signed out / forged token:* every route is in `authenticate("auth-jwt")` → 401, tested for GET, POST, PATCH and DELETE, plus a forged token.
  - *Another user's limit (IDOR):* every lookup and mutation is `WHERE id = ? AND user_id = <token user>`; another user's id is 404 (route test), and the Postgres repository is tested for it.
  - *CSRF:* cookie is `SameSite=Strict`; route tests send the app's `Origin` on mutations so they keep passing when AOS-618 adds an Origin check. Bodies go through the AOS-615 size cap (a 2 MiB body → 413, tested on the lens route).
  - *Hostile input:* amounts must match `\d{1,8}(\.\d{1,2})?`, be > 0 and ≤ ₹1 crore (DB check too); scope kind, lens id, category (no transfer), period and merchant (1..255) are validated → 400 naming the field; lens ids in paths are checked against the shipped file. SQL-injection and `<script>` merchant text is stored and returned as text (route test; Playwright checks it renders inert).
  - *Notification abuse / spam:* at most one push per (limit, period, threshold) by primary key, race-tested with ten concurrent claims on Postgres; the alert switch and quiet hours apply.
  - *Logs:* only ids and percentages (`Limit {id} reached 80% for user {id}`); never amounts or merchant names.
- **Data at rest:** limit labels/amounts the owner chose; same sensitivity as existing analytics (no new secrets, no raw text). Not encrypted, justified as for transactions.
- **Listeners / dependencies:** none new.

## Proposed decision D-83 — Limits: a learning month, suggested limits the owner accepts, one push per threshold per period
2026-10-18 · Proposed (owner's answers 2026-10-17: learning month, suggestions, nothing pushes unaccepted) · Type: Product / Architecture · Tasks: AOS-904 · Builds on D-46, D-50, D-51, D-80, D-81, D-82
- **Learning month:** no limits for the first 30 days of data (from the first spend's local day, inclusive); the page says "Learning your habits: N of 30 days"; creating a limit is 409 until then.
- **Suggestions:** per lens, weekly = median of the last 4 complete Mon–Sun weeks − 10%, rounded down to ₹50, at least ₹100; only with spending in ≥ 2 of those weeks, no limit on that lens, no "Not now" in 30 days. Shown with its working. Accepting (as is or adjusted) creates an ordinary limit; **only accepted limits push**.
- **Limits:** scope = lens, merchant or category; weekly (Mon–Sun) or monthly, in the user's zone; one per scope and period. Person-to-person UPI never counts for lenses or categories (D-81); a merchant limit counts that merchant exactly.
- **Alerts:** 50/80/100%. A ledger row per (limit, period start, threshold), primary key = the claim; claim then send (at most once). Several thresholds at once → one push for the highest. Thresholds already passed when a limit is set or changed are recorded silently. A refund that drops spending below a threshold does not re-arm it in that period. Alerts off → thresholds still recorded silently (no backlog when switched on). Checked after each new payment and every 10 minutes. Coach pushes are `NotificationType.COACH`, gated by `coach_settings`, quiet hours as usual.
- **Rejected:** flags on the limit reset per period (a reset job to keep right); looking for earlier notifications (per channel, prunable); a listener only (misses re-categorisation and lens edits); pushing suggestions (the owner hasn't agreed to them).
