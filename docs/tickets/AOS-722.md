## Description
Let the owner confirm or correct the salary the forecast detected (payer and usual day), and remember the answer.

## Why
A follow-up from AOS-714 (month-end forecast and money left until salary, D-76): the salary is detected only; a wrong payer or day makes "money left until salary" wrong with no way to fix it.

## Scope
- **In:** a confirm/correct action on the Home forecast card; a stored choice that detection respects; an API route.
- **Out:** several salaries or irregular income.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Confirming keeps the detected payer and day; correcting stores the chosen payer and day, and the forecast uses them.
- [ ] The route requires a session (route inventory), rejects missing CSRF/Origin, and another user's data is 404.
- [ ] Playwright: the card shows the confirmed salary; untrusted payer names render as text.
