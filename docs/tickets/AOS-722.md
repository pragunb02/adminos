## Description
Let the owner confirm or correct the salary the forecast detected (payer and usual day), and remember the answer.

## Why
A follow-up from AOS-714 (month-end forecast and money left until salary, D-76): the salary is detected only; a wrong payer or day makes "money left until salary" wrong with no way to fix it.

## Scope
- **In:** a confirm/correct action on the Home forecast card; a stored choice that detection respects; an API route.
- **Out:** several salaries or irregular income.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Confirming keeps the detected payer and day; correcting stores the chosen payer and day, and the forecast uses them (`SalaryChoiceServiceTest`, `SalaryChoiceDetectionTest`, `MonthEndForecasterTest`, `ForecastServiceTest`).
- [x] The route requires a session (route inventory), rejects missing CSRF/Origin, and another user's data is never visible or changed (`RouteInventoryTest`, `SalaryChoiceSecurityTest`, `IdorTest`; the route names no id, so "404" is "Bob sees no choice of Alice's and his PUT only writes his own row").
- [x] Playwright: the card shows the confirmed salary; untrusted payer names render as text (`e2e/salary-confirm.spec.ts`).

## Security
D-100 threat review (ENGINEERING §4a).
- **What it exposes:**
  - two session routes, `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/forecast/salary`, inside `authenticate("auth-jwt")`; the route-inventory test passes with no new allowlist entry;
  - new stored data: `salary_choices` (V93), one row per user (payer, day, amount, kind, times), deleted with the user;
  - `GET /api/v1/forecast` `salary` gains `source` and `usualDay`; `reason` may be `not_seen`.
  - No new upload, outbound call, job, secret, dependency or listener.
- **Abuse and what stops it:**
  - **A stranger on the internet / a forged or expired token:** 401 (`SalaryRoutesTest`, route inventory); the web goes to login (Playwright).
  - **A cross-site page riding the owner's cookie (CSRF):** the global `OriginCheck` refuses a missing or foreign Origin and cross-site / same-site Fetch Metadata with 403 `CSRF_001`, and nothing is stored (`SalaryChoiceSecurityTest`). The route reads JSON only (415 `VALIDATION_005` otherwise, D-102, `RequestBodyErrorsRoutesTest`), so a preflight-free form or text post can't reach it.
  - **Another user's data (IDOR):** no id in the path or body; the user is the session's only and a `userId` in the body is ignored (test). Bob's GET shows no choice of Alice's; Bob's confirm and correct leave Alice's row unchanged and write only his own (`IdorTest`).
  - **Hostile input:** the amount must match `\d{1,10}(\.\d{1,2})?` before it is parsed (no sign, exponent, grouping or Devanagari digits), > 0 and ≤ ₹100 crore; the day is 1–31; the payer is trimmed, 1–100 characters, with no control, format (bidi overrides, zero-width), separator, surrogate, unassigned or private-use characters. Errors are 400 `VALIDATION_001` and never echo the input. Confirm takes no values from the client (a body with values is 400); a detected payer that can't be stored is 409 `SALARY_002`. The database repeats the checks (the integration test inserts bad rows directly). Printable hostile strings (`<script>`, SQL) are stored as bound values and returned as JSON text.
  - **Hostile text in the browser:** the payer (from bank narrations, untrusted) is rendered as a text node only (Vitest + Playwright: `<img src=x onerror=…>` shows as text, no element, the handler never runs).
  - **A stolen session:** it could change the salary the forecast uses; limited to 20 changes a minute (429 `RATE_001`), it reads nothing of other users, and no money moves. The owner sees "Corrected by you" on the card.
  - **Oversized bodies:** the global 1 MB limit (413) applies before the body is read.
- **Values in logs:** nothing is logged; `SalaryChoice.toString()` has no payer or amount (test). No payer or amount in local/session storage or the URL (Playwright).
- **Data at rest:** payer and amount are plaintext, justified: the same employer name and salary amount already sit in plaintext in `transactions` (`merchant_name`, `amount`), which they are copied from and compared with, as do `bills.biller_name` and `mandates.payee`. Encryption in this repo is for people's names and free text (Splitwise, Ask feedback) and for data not stored elsewhere (balances, D-170).
- **AI:** none; deterministic (D-67).
- **Secrets / fixtures:** synthetic payers and amounts only.

## Resolution
- **Model and storage:** `SalaryChoice` (payer or none, day 1–31, amount > 0 and ≤ ₹100 crore, kind confirmed/corrected), one row per user in `salary_choices` (V93), `SalaryChoiceRepository` (Exposed + in-memory).
- **Detection respects the choice (`SalaryDetector.detect(credits, today, choice)`):**
  - the chosen payer's credits of at least half the chosen amount count; payers match loosely in any script, as whole words in order ("acme" matches "ACME SALARY OCT", not "acmecorp"); no payer matches only nameless credits; none in the last 200 days → unknown `not_seen`;
  - the newest is the last salary (money left starts from it); the weekend habit is the one under which the chosen day explains most of the payer's credits; late and stopped as for detection; the amount is the owner's;
  - two salaries: the choice says which payer is the salary; a new employer or a raise: a correction moves the forecast at once.
  - Without a choice detection is unchanged (`SalaryDetectorTest` untouched).
- **Service and routes:** `SalaryChoiceService` (`view`, `confirm`, `correct`); `GET /api/v1/forecast/salary` (what detection alone finds, its reason, and the stored choice) and `PUT` `{"action":"confirm"}` (stores exactly what was detected: payer, usual day before any weekend move, typical amount; 409 `SALARY_001` when nothing is detected, `SALARY_002` when the detected payer can't be stored) or `{"action":"correct","payer","day","amount"}`; 20 changes a minute per user.
- **Forecast:** `ForecastService` reads the user's choice into `ForecastInput.salaryChoice`; `salary` in `GET /forecast` gains `source` (`detected`/`confirmed`) and `usualDay`.
- **Web:** `SalaryConfirm` ("Your salary") inside the salary "How we got this" (expected and late) and in the card when no salary is known: "Yes, that's right" (only while nothing is stored) and "Correct it" (payer, day of month, amount, checked as the server does); a "confirmed" badge beside the salary line.
- **Tests:** `SalaryChoiceDetectionTest` (27), `SalaryChoiceServiceTest` (9), `SalaryRoutesTest` (14), `SalaryChoiceSecurityTest` (4), `SalaryChoiceRepositoryIntegrationTest` (4), new cases in `MonthEndForecasterTest`, `ForecastServiceTest`, `ForecastRoutesTest`, `IdorTest`, `RequestBodyErrorsRoutesTest`; Vitest `salary.test.ts` (29), `SalaryConfirm.test.tsx` (12), `ForecastCard.test.tsx` (+5); Playwright `e2e/salary-confirm.spec.ts` (11 × 3 projects, attack specs included).
- **Known behaviour:** a choice of a day earlier than the last credit's (credits on the 28th, chosen day the 1st) reads that credit as the next month's salary paid early, so the next payday is the month after (the careful side for money left). Confirming a month-end pattern stores its nominal day (the 31st, clamped in short months).
- **Found, not fixed:** `SalaryDetector.payerKey` keeps only a–z and 0–9, so non-Latin payer names collapse into one key during detection (the choice path uses its own Unicode-aware key).
