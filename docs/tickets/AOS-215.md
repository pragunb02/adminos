## Description
`BankSmsParser` decided direction by the earliest verb and ignored `Dr.`/`Cr.`. "A/c XX1357 Dr. Rs 500.00 … credited to
VPA swiggy@icici" became a **credit** of Rs 500; "INR 750.00 Cr to A/c … for the order you paid" became a **debit**.

## Why
A wrong direction is worse than a gap (D-43): a debit read as income both hides spending and inflates income.

## Scope
- **In:** direction for every bank SMS/WhatsApp parser (`BankSmsParser`, shared `SmsSignals`).
- **Out:** Gmail's Go `detectDirection` (already D-43); statement imports (columns give direction).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] An explicit Dr/Cr marker on an amount ("Dr. Rs 500", "INR 750.00 Cr", "is Dr. with", "Cr. with") decides, over a payee's "credited" or a "paid"/"sent" word
- [x] An account-anchored phrase ("debited from … A/c", "A/c XX1234 debited", "credited to your … account") decides over unanchored words
- [x] Strong signals that disagree, or verbs that disagree with no strong signal, give `Ignored("direction unclear, needs review")`; nothing is guessed
- [x] Existing HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak and generic debits, credits, refunds and mandate debits behave as before
- [x] "Dr." in a name ("Dr. Mehta Clinic") and "credit card"/"debit card" are not direction signals

## Design
- **Context:** D-43 (email) says what happened to *your* account wins; otherwise one-sided words decide; otherwise skip
  and count as needs-review. SMS had no needs-review notion.
- **Decision:** `SmsSignals.direction` returns `Clear(direction)`, `Conflicting` or `None`:
  1. strong: account-anchored phrases (both orders) and Dr/Cr markers next to an amount; agreement decides;
  2. otherwise verbs (debited, spent, sent, paid, withdrawn, purchase, deducted, debit… / credited, received,
     deposited, refund, reversed, credit…), if one-sided;
  3. disagreement at the deciding level is `Conflicting`.
  Balance and limit figures are removed first, so "Avl Bal Rs 4,500 Cr" never votes. A parser returns
  `SmsReading.DirectionUnclear` (only when there is an amount); the registry reports it as
  `Ignored("direction unclear, needs review")` (constant `SmsParseResult.DIRECTION_UNCLEAR`), so the raw-SMS route
  and the WhatsApp import count it by that reason. `SmsParser.read` has a default built on `parse`, so existing parsers
  and tests are unchanged.
- **Options considered for needs-review:** 1) a new `needs_review` status in the raw-SMS response (contract change for
  the Mac reader and the web); 2) an `Ignored` reason, as Gmail skips and counts (chosen; additive, no contract change).
- **Impact:** domain only. No schema, route or contract change.

**Proposed D-119 (Type: Business logic; Tasks: AOS-215, builds on D-43):** SMS and WhatsApp direction comes from
account-anchored phrases and Dr/Cr markers first, then one-sided verbs; when the deciding signals disagree, the message
is skipped with reason `direction unclear, needs review`, never guessed. This replaces "the earliest verb wins".
Rejected: earliest verb (the bug); first anchored phrase wins (guesses on "debited from A/c X and credited to A/c Y").

## Security
- **Exposes:** nothing new. **Abuse:** a crafted SMS can't force a wrong direction past a strong signal; mixed signals
  are skipped (fail safe, no money invented). Regexes are bounded; tested on 2,000-character hostile input.
- Reasons never quote the text (D-33). Fixtures are synthetic.

## Verification
- Before: "A/c XX1357 Dr. Rs 500.00 … credited to VPA …" `CREDIT 500.00`; "INR 750.00 Cr to A/c … you paid" `DEBIT 750.00`;
  "Rs.3,000 Cr. … sent from ACME" `DEBIT 3000.00`; "A/c credited with Rs 500.00 Dr" `CREDIT 500.00`;
  "debited from A/c XX5678 and credited to A/c XX9999" `DEBIT 5000.00`.
  After: `DEBIT 500.00`, `CREDIT 750.00`, `CREDIT 3000.00`, and the last two `Ignored(direction unclear, needs review)`.
- Gates as in AOS-213.

## Risks / notes
- Behaviour change: a transfer SMS naming both your account and the payee's account as anchored ("debited from A/c X
  and credited to A/c Y") is now needs-review instead of a debit. It is counted, not silently lost.
- Verb-only conflicts ("Refund of Rs 249 for the order you paid … is processed") are now needs-review instead of the
  earliest verb.
- When AOS-705 (PR #121) merges, needs-review messages could go to its Review queue instead of being only counted.
