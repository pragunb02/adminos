## Description
A pure engine that turns one account's ledger rows into FIFO lots per holding, with realised gains per disposal, unrealised gains at a price, dividends and taxes, in the instrument's currency and in rupees; and the split of a foreign INR gain into price and FX effects.

## Why
D-79: lots and holdings are derived from the append-only ledger and rebuildable. Gains and "invested" on the Wealth screen (AOS-820) and returns (AOS-812) come from here.

## Scope
- **In:** `domain/wealth/lots/LotEngine.kt` (lots, disposals, issues, `GainSplit`), `LotService`, tests.
- **Out:** grandfathering (31 Jan 2018 FMV) and tax classes (AOS-813); the broker's specific-lot choice for foreign shares (open question 3 in the research); TWR (AOS-812).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] FIFO: sales and switches out consume the oldest lots; each consumed piece has its own cost, proceeds share (by units) and gain; cost is conserved exactly (golden cases + a 200-trial seeded property test).
- [x] Stamp duty is part of the cost of that day's purchases (units are allotted on the net amount); a stray stamp row is tracked separately.
- [x] Corporate actions: bonus = new lot at cost 0 on its date; split = units change, cost and dates don't; scheme merger = cost and original dates carried into the new scheme; IDCW reinvestment = a purchase; side pocket / gift in without a cost = cost 0, flagged.
- [x] Reversals (bounced SIP) remove the newest units with no gain; transfers out remove the oldest with no gain.
- [x] Overselling is flagged (`OVERSOLD`), never a negative lot.
- [x] Multi-currency: cost and proceeds in the instrument currency and in INR (each at its own date's rate, from the rows); `GainSplit` gives price effect + FX effect that add up exactly.
- [x] Dividends paid out and taxes (TDS, STT) are tracked, not costs.

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Stored lots vs derived on read:** (a) a `lots` table with a ledger version, rebuilt on change (research §3.1); (b) **derive on read**. At personal scale (hundreds of rows) derivation is microseconds, and there is no second copy to keep in step with undo and corrections. Chosen (b); a cache can be added behind `LotService` if it ever matters.
  2. **Exactness on partial lots:** (a) cost per unit × units (rounds every sale); (b) **proportional cost at 34 significant digits, scaled to 10 places, with the remainder kept on the lot**, so cost + disposed cost = the purchase cost exactly. Chosen (b).
  3. **Mergers:** (a) treat as a sale and a purchase (wrong for tax, resets holding periods); (b) **carry cost and dates within the account on the same day**. Chosen (b); an unmatched merger in is flagged.
- **Decision:** proposed **D-163** below. **Patterns:** pure function over the ledger (deterministic, easily tested); value objects.
- **Extensibility:** a new event type is one branch in `apply`; tax lot rules (grandfathering) can wrap the disposal list in AOS-813.
- **Impact:** domain only; `LotService` in Koin.

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** nothing new: no routes, storage, jobs or outbound calls; it reads the user's own active ledger.
- **Abuse:** hostile or impossible rows are refused by the model (AOS-802) and imports block holdings that don't reconcile (AOS-805); the engine never produces a negative lot or a silent wrong cost (issues are returned and shown).
- **Data at rest:** none new. **Dependencies:** none.

## Proposed decision D-163 (for the integrator)
### D-163 — Lots are derived from the ledger on read: FIFO, exact proportional cost, stamp duty in cost, mergers carry cost and dates
2026-10-18 · Proposed · Type: Architecture · Tasks: AOS-811 · Builds on D-79
- Lots and gains are computed from the active ledger each time (no lots table); same rows → same lots.
- FIFO per holding; a partial lot's cost is proportional at 34 significant digits (10 places), the remainder stays on the lot, so costs are conserved exactly; display rounds.
- Stamp duty adds to that day's purchase cost; bonus = cost 0 lot on its date; split = units only; merger = cost and dates carried within the account; reversal = newest units out, no gain; gift out = oldest out, no gain; missing costs and oversells are flagged issues, never guessed.
- Each disposal keeps cost and proceeds in the instrument currency and INR (each side at its own rate from the rows); `GainSplit` reports price effect (P₁−P₀)·Q·FX₀ and FX effect P₁·Q·(FX₁−FX₀).
- **Rejected:** a stored lots table now (a second source of truth to keep in step with undo); cost-per-unit arithmetic (rounding drift); mergers as sale + purchase.
