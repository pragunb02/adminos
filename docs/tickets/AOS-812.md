## Description
Money-weighted return (XIRR) per holding and portfolio, exact (`BigDecimal`, no `Double`), with a bracketed solver that never shows an unconverged number; "not defined" when no rate (or more than one) fits; absolute return under one year (SEBI/AMFI convention); Modified Dietz and chain-linking for time-weighted comparison with a benchmark.

## Why
D-79 rejects Excel-style Newton XIRR (divergence, wrong roots). The Wealth screen (AOS-820) shows XIRR next to gains; guidance (AOS-818) needs returns it can trust.

## Scope
- **In:** `domain/wealth/returns/BigMath.kt` (ln, exp, pow on BigDecimal), `Returns.kt` (`Xirr`, `Returns.cashFlows`, `Returns.of` → `ReturnFigure`, `ModifiedDietz`), tests.
- **Out:** a benchmark index series (needs index prices, later); INR-vs-instrument-currency XIRR for foreign holdings is supported by the cash-flow function's `amountInr`, the instrument-currency variant comes with AOS-807.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] XIRR is Actual/365 and bracketed over [−99.99%, +1,000%]: the bracket is scanned, exactly one sign change → bisection to 1e-11; none → "not defined" (no sign change / out of range); several → "several rates fit, none shown"; all flows on one day or fewer than two → "not defined" (tests).
- [x] Matches known values: Excel's documented example (37.336253…%), the research's worked example (20.2557%), ±10% over one year exactly (tests to 9–10 places; references computed independently at 60 digits).
- [x] NPV at the found rate is ≈ 0 within ₹0.01 (seeded 60-trial property test); 20 years of monthly SIPs solve in well under a second.
- [x] Returns under a year are shown as absolute; from a year, the annual XIRR (`ReturnFigure.basis`).
- [x] Cash flows by type: purchases, stamp duty, STT, TDS out; redemptions and gross dividends in; reinvestments, bonuses, splits, mergers, transfers no cash; a reversal returns money when it removes units (test).
- [x] Modified Dietz and chain-linking (TWR) with tests (the research's 20.5% example).

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Solver:** (a) Newton–Raphson (Excel); (b) Brent; (c) **scan + bisection**. (a) rejected by D-79; (b) is faster but the scan is what detects "several rates fit" honestly, and bisection after it is simple and always converges. Chosen (c).
  2. **Arithmetic:** (a) `Double` for the search; (b) **BigDecimal with our own ln/exp** (50 significant digits). D-79 says never Double; (b) is fast enough because `(1 + r)^(−d/365) = vᵈ` needs one exp per rate and an integer power per flow.
  3. **Short periods:** (a) always annualise; (b) **absolute under a year** (SEBI/AMFI convention; +3% in 20 days is not "71% a year"). Chosen (b); the XIRR is still computed and available.
- **Decision:** proposed **D-132** below. **Patterns:** pure functions; sealed result types (`XirrResult`).
- **Extensibility:** a benchmark comparison plugs index valuations into `ModifiedDietz`; another day-count would be a parameter.
- **Impact:** domain only.

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** nothing new (no routes, storage, calls). **Abuse:** pathological flows (huge, tiny, many sign changes) end as "not defined", never an exception or a wrong number shown as fact; computation is bounded (fixed grid, ≤ 200 bisection steps). **Data:** none stored. **Dependencies:** none.

## Proposed decision D-132 (for the integrator)
### D-132 — XIRR: BigDecimal, Actual/365, scan then bisect in [−99.99%, +1,000%]; absolute under a year
2026-10-18 · Proposed · Type: Business logic · Tasks: AOS-812 · Builds on D-79
- Cash flows from the investor's side in rupees: purchases, stamp duty, STT and TDS out; redemptions and gross dividends in; no cash for reinvestment, bonus, split, merger, transfer; the current value as a final inflow.
- The rate is searched in [−99.99%, +1,000%] on a fixed grid; exactly one sign change → bisection to 1e-11 (10 places shown as 1 decimal); none → "not defined" (with the reason); more than one → "several rates fit, none shown". No Newton's method; never an unconverged number. All maths in BigDecimal (50 digits).
- Under one year since the first investment, the screen shows the absolute return (gain ÷ money put in); from a year, the XIRR. Modified Dietz and chain-linking are for benchmark comparison.
- **Rejected:** Newton/Excel XIRR; Double arithmetic; annualising short periods.
