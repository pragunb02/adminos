## Description
One place for everything to pay, and reminders you can trust:
- a **month view** of bills and expected subscription renewals with totals;
- **renewal reminders**;
- fixes for the reminder bugs found by the E5 survey, and the Gmail bills that broke a whole batch.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **Calendar:** `GET /api/v1/dues?month=YYYY-MM` (default: this month, the user's date) returns:
  - bills by due date (cancelled ones excluded);
  - renewals projected from each active or trial subscription's next billing date, by cycle, from today on and at most a year ahead. Daily subscriptions show only the next date, and a renewal on the 31st stays on month ends;
  - overdue bills from earlier months, with the current month only;
  - totals: to pay, paid, expected renewals, as exact money strings;
  - a 400 for a malformed month or one more than 5 years from today.
- [x] **Web `/bills` → "Dues & renewals":**
  - the month is in the URL (`?m=`): previous/next push it, "this month" drops it, and back/forward/refresh keep it;
  - totals, an overdue section, and days with their bills and renewals ("Due tomorrow", "Paid", "Expected", "Trial ends"), worked out from the **user's** today as returned by the API, not the browser clock;
  - loading, empty, and error-with-retry states.
- [x] **Reminders on the user's own dates (`UserCalendar`):**
  - "N days before" and "overdue" use the user's time zone; a missing or invalid zone falls back to India time;
  - a bill isn't marked overdue while it's still its due date where the user is.
- [x] **Days already sent are stored (V21):**
  - a custom reminder day (e.g. 14 or 7) is sent once, even across restarts;
  - the look-ahead now matches preferences (up to 30 days; it used to stop at 7).
- [x] **Renewal reminders:**
  - "Netflix renews in 3 days" or "Free trial of X ends in 1 day", on the bill-reminder days, with "₹649.00 expected on …, based on your past payments.";
  - once per (renewal date, day); a new renewal date starts afresh;
  - none for cancelled, paused or undated subscriptions, or when reminders are off.
- [x] **Gmail bills:**
  - due dates are normalised to ISO with Indian day-first parsing (Postgres would have read 05/11 as 11 May);
  - a bill with no readable due date is skipped and counted as failed, instead of failing every email in the batch;
  - the bill type is guessed from whole words (so "premium" isn't "emi"), and the card or account's last 4 digits are saved, which AOS-508 uses to match payments.
- [x] **Postgres keeps subscription metadata on update,** the same bug as bills in AOS-508.

## Design
- **Options for the calendar:**
  1. **One API that merges bills and projected renewals** (chosen). It is one place for the rules, and the web only renders.
  2. **The web joins `/bills` and `/subscriptions`.** That duplicates the projection rules in TypeScript.
  3. **Store renewal rows.** That is stale data to keep in sync with detection.
- **Options for the reminder state:**
  1. **Columns** (V21; chosen). They are queryable and visible.
  2. **Metadata JSON:** hidden, and dropped by the old update bug.
- **Patterns:**
  - `UserCalendar`, a small shared service;
  - `RenewalReminders`, separate from `ReminderEngine` (single responsibility, at most 4 dependencies);
  - pure projection (`DuesService.renewalDates`).
- **Extensibility:** another due source (e.g. EMIs or insurance premiums from statements) is one more mapping into `DueItem` in `DuesService`.
- **Impact:**
  - API: `DuesService`, `/dues`, `UserCalendar`, `RenewalReminders`, `ReminderEngine` (user dates, stored days, 30-day look-ahead), `BillRemindersJob` (sweep → bills → renewals), `SubscriptionRepository.findRenewingBetween`, V21;
  - worker: the Gmail bill insert (type, account ref, ISO date);
  - web: the `/bills` page, types and fixtures.

## Risks / notes
- **Renewal dates are predictions** from past payments (subscription detection, AOS-501), and the copy says so.
- **Renewal reminders use the bill-reminder setting and days.** Separate renewal settings can come later if wanted.
- **Not stored as bills:** EMIs, insurance premiums and credit-card statements seen only in SMS still aren't bills (statement and SMS bill detection is a later task).
