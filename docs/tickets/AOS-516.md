## Description
Two accuracy bugs in `SubscriptionDetectorService`:
1. **Too broad.** Any merchant charged at a steady interval became a subscription: a weekly Zepto shop, a monthly BigBasket order, a fuel top-up, an electricity bill, a SIP or an LIC premium.
2. **Plan switches.** After a monthly → yearly switch, the detector stayed on the monthly cycle. When the next yearly charge came, the mixed intervals matched no cycle and the subscription was marked ended ("No charge since …"). The reverse switch was read as a yearly plan with a 90% price cut. AOS-715 (PR #85) already treats a charge 8–14× the usual one as a plan switch, but nothing moved the cycle.

## Why
Wrong subscriptions feed the waste score (D-58), the dues calendar and renewal reminders (D-55), and the cost totals. A real subscription marked ended after a plan switch drops out of all of them.

## Scope
- **In:**
  - subscription-like signals (`SubscriptionSignals`): a catalogue service subcategory, a prepaid plan, a mandate/AutoPay, stable amounts; never everyday shopping or a financial product;
  - everyday shopping from the catalogue **and** the habit lenses, through the `EverydaySpending` port (`LensEverydaySpending`);
  - plan-switch detection (`PlanSwitch`) that moves the same subscription to the new cycle;
  - a synthetic eval set with a committed baseline and a no-regression test;
  - `./gradlew subscriptionEvalReport [-PwriteBaseline]`.
- **Out:**
  - person-to-person rent and society maintenance (see Risks);
  - the price-rise rule itself (AOS-715);
  - schema changes: there is no migration.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A steady interval alone isn't a subscription. These are never subscriptions:
  - groceries, food or quick commerce (catalogue subcategory groceries/food_delivery/restaurant/cafe, a food category, or the quick-commerce/food-delivery lens), unless a mandate pays it (a membership on AutoPay);
  - loans, investments, insurance and wallets, even with a mandate (D-56);
  - amounts that vary. One price change is allowed. A known service or a mandate may also rise in several steps, as long as the price only goes up.
- [x] These are subscriptions when the amounts are stable:
  - a known service (streaming, music, cloud, apps, books, news, gaming, fitness, courses, or category subscription);
  - a prepaid plan (mobile, broadband, TV);
  - anything paid by a mandate;
  - an unknown merchant that isn't categorised as food, shopping or transport (a local gym).
- [x] A merchant the catalogue knows as something else (fuel, electricity, rail, pharmacy, movies…) isn't a subscription unless a mandate pays it.
- [x] A charge 8–14× (or 1/14–1/8 of) both the median of the up-to-3 charges before it and the charge just before it is a plan switch:
  - the same subscription row moves to the new cycle (monthly ↔ yearly), stays active and isn't ended;
  - `priceChanged` compares only charges on the current plan, so a switch is never a "+900%" rise;
  - `metadata.planSwitchedFrom` / `planSwitchedOn` are set.
- [x] A switch is only believed when the charges before it ran on the opposite cycle. Otherwise the history is read as a whole, as before.
- [x] The eval set is synthetic, has ≥ 20 subscriptions and ≥ 20 regular-shopping cases, and its baseline is committed. `SubscriptionEvalTest` fails if precision, recall, F1 or cycle accuracy drop.
- [x] D-65 behaviour is unchanged otherwise: 400-day look-back, user dates, calendar next charge, catalogue category, ended after two cycles + 7 days, a new charge reactivates.

## Design
- **Context:**
  - `detectSubscriptions` grouped debits by merchant, read a cycle from the intervals, and saved every group with a cycle.
  - The habit lenses (AOS-901) already say what quick commerce and food delivery are.
  - The financial domain must not depend on the coach (ENGINEERING §1).
- **Options considered:**
  1. **A score with weights.** Rejected: hard to explain, and a sum lets a strong "same amount" outweigh "it's a grocery shop".
  2. **An ordered decision list of named signals**, the first matching rule wins, with a reason for each outcome. **Chosen.**
  3. **Require a catalogue service or a mandate for everything.** Rejected: an unknown local gym or a newspaper paid by card would never be found (a recall drop).
  4. **Plan switches:** create a second subscription on the new cycle and end the old one. Rejected: it loses the user's marks (used/unused, kept factors), and the "ended" row is wrong. Chosen instead: move the same row (unique key `(user, merchant, cycle)` is respected, because the new-cycle row is looked up first).
- **Decision (proposed D-126):** the decision list below; everyday shopping = catalogue ∪ lenses through a port; a plan switch moves the cycle of the same subscription.
  1. financial → no
  2. amounts vary → no
  3. everyday and no mandate → no
  4. known service / plan / mandate → yes
  5. a catalogue merchant of another kind → no
  6. food, shopping or transport category → no
  7. otherwise → yes
- **Patterns:**
  - Specification (`SubscriptionSignals.assess`);
  - Port and adapter (`EverydaySpending`, implemented by `CatalogueEverydaySpending` and the coach's `LensEverydaySpending`, combined with `anyOf`).
- **Extensibility:** a new signal is one enum value and one line in `assess`. A new everyday source is one more `EverydaySpending`.
- **Impact:**
  - `domain/financial/SubscriptionSignals.kt` (new);
  - `SubscriptionDetectorService.kt` (`readCycle`, signals, cycle move);
  - `domain/coach/lenses/LensEverydaySpending.kt` (new);
  - `di/CoachKoinModule.kt` (`subscriptionDetector` wiring) and `di/AppModule.kt` (one line);
  - `build.gradle.kts` (`subscriptionEvalReport` task);
  - tests and `src/test/resources/subscriptions/{eval.csv,baseline.json}`.
  - No route, DTO or schema change.

## Eval (synthetic, 51 cases: 27 subscriptions, 24 regular payments; replayed night by night up to "today")
| | Precision | Recall | F1 | Cycle accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (main) | 0.5417 (26/48) | 0.963 (26/27) | 0.6934 | 0.8846 (23/26) |
| After (baseline) | 0.931 (27/29) | 1.0 (27/27) | 0.9643 | 1.0 (27/27) |

- **Before:**
  - 22 false positives: every grocery, food, quick-commerce, café, transport, fuel, electricity, insurance, SIP, e-commerce, pharmacy, rail and movies case;
  - three plan switches on the wrong cycle;
  - one plan switch (renewed yearly after monthly) missed and marked ended.
- **After, known misses (both left honestly in the set):**
  - N21: monthly rent to a person by UPI;
  - N22: a society maintenance bill from an unknown merchant.
  - Both are stable and regular, at merchants the catalogue doesn't know. Fixing them needs person-to-person detection in the financial domain, or a "bill" signal. That's a follow-up, not guessed here.

## Security (§4a)
- **Exposure:** none new. No route, input, upload, outbound call, secret or dependency. The detector runs in the existing nightly job over the owner's own transactions.
- **Abuse:** a forged SMS could already create debits. With this change it gets *harder* for such debits to become a subscription, because they now need stable amounts and a service, plan or mandate signal.
  - The mandate signal comes from the existing mandate matcher and SMS parser (reviewed in AOS-502), or from words in the narration.
  - A narration that says "AUTOPAY" can still make a stable charge at an unknown merchant look like a subscription. It was already one before this change (any steady interval was), so that's no worse.
- **Logs:** the rejection log is at debug level with the user ID and the reason only, never the merchant name or amounts (D-33).
- **Tests:** hostile and edge input in the unit tests:
  - token and zero charges;
  - 15× and 5× ratios;
  - a promo charge in between;
  - whole-word narration matching ("MANDATEX" isn't a mandate);
  - empty inputs refused.
- **Playwright:** no new screen, so no new spec.

## Risks / notes
- **Bug found, not fixed (outside this step):** a merchant group that is still detected is never ended. `detectSubscriptions` re-saves every group with a cycle as `ACTIVE`, even when its last charge was months ago. `endStopped` then skips it because it is "still charging". So a Netflix cancelled 5 months ago, with earlier charges inside the 400-day window, stays active with a past next-billing date, which contradicts D-65. The fix: a group whose last charge is older than two cycles + 7 days isn't "still charging". Proposed as a follow-up task.
- **Previously detected false positives** (e.g. a weekly Zepto "subscription" in the owner's database) are no longer updated. D-65's rule ends them after two cycles + 7 days with "No charge since …". That wording is slightly off for these rows, but nothing is deleted.
- **AOS-715 (PR #85)** is open and replaces the `priceChanged` rule with `PriceChangeRule`. When it is rebased onto this change:
  - it should compare `reading.current` (the charges on the current plan);
  - it should share `PlanSwitch.RATIO`, the same 8–14 band.

## Proposed decision D-126 (not yet in DECISIONS.md)
**Subscription detection needs subscription-like signals; a plan switch moves the cycle.** Type: Product / Architecture. Tasks: AOS-516, AOS-715. Builds on D-56, D-58, D-65, D-81.
- **Steady intervals aren't enough.** An ordered decision list of named signals decides, and each outcome has a reason.
  - Financial products are never subscriptions (D-56).
  - Varying amounts aren't. One change is allowed, or several rises for a known service or a mandate.
  - Everyday shopping isn't (catalogue ∪ quick-commerce/food-delivery lenses), unless a mandate pays it.
  - Known services, prepaid plans and mandates are.
  - Other catalogue kinds, and food/shopping/transport categories, aren't.
  - An unknown merchant at a stable price is.
- **Everyday shopping is a port** (`EverydaySpending`) in the financial domain. The coach's lenses implement it, so the detector and the leaks page agree.
- **A plan switch** is a charge 8–14× (or the reverse) both the recent median and the previous charge, with the opposite cycle before it. It moves the same subscription to the new cycle; it is never a price change and never "ended".
- **Measured** on a synthetic eval set with a committed baseline that may not drop.
- **Rejected:**
  - a weighted score (opaque);
  - requiring a catalogue service or mandate (misses local gyms);
  - a new row per cycle (loses the user's marks).
