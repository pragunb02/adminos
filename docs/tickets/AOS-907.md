## Description
Goals on a habit lens: **"cut <lens> by X% vs the last 3 months"** or **"keep <lens> under ₹Y"**, each weekly or monthly, with progress per period and a no-spend-day streak for the lens. Shown on the leaks page (set, see, delete) and on Home.

## Why
D-80 / BRD §5.6: "cut X by 30%" with streaks; streaks are the one game mechanic D-80 keeps.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A cut goal's baseline is the lens's spend over the 3 full calendar months before it was set, per week (× 7 ÷ days) or per month (÷ 3), frozen; target = baseline × (100 − X)%, rounded down to the rupee. X is 1..90.
- [x] A cut with no spending in those months is refused (409, "set a cap instead"); a cap goal is the amount per period.
- [x] Progress: every period from the goal's start to today (at most 12), spent (refunds net off) vs target, met or over; the current one "so far".
- [x] Streak: days in a row with no order in the lens ending today (0 if there was one today), and the best run in the last year of data; refunds don't break a run; days before the first data don't count.
- [x] One goal per lens; another user's goal is 404; unknown lens 404; bad kind/period/value 400.
- [x] The leaks page lists goals with past periods in words (✓ met / ✗ over, never colour alone), sets and deletes them; Home shows each goal's period and streak (nothing without goals).

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Goals as limits with a flag:** reuses alerts, but goals don't push and a cut target depends on a frozen baseline; it would mix two contracts.
  2. **A separate goal model with a frozen baseline and pure progress maths** (chosen): `Goal` stores its target; `GoalMath` computes periods and streaks from the same leak facts as everything else.
  3. **A moving baseline (always the last 3 months):** the target drifts as spending falls, so success raises the bar; rejected.
- **Decision:** option 2. **Patterns:** value object + pure functions (`GoalMath`), Repository (in-memory + Exposed).
- **Extensibility:** another goal kind (e.g. "at most N orders") is one `GoalKind` and one target rule.
- **Impact:** `domain/coach/goals/*`, `V43__coach_goals.sql` (tentative), web `lib/goals.ts`, `components/goals/{GoalsPanel,GoalsHomeCard}.tsx`, the leaks page and Home.

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** `GET/POST /coach/goals`, `DELETE /coach/goals/{id}` (authenticated); one table.
- **Abuse:** signed out → 401 for all three (route test); another user's goal id → 404 (`WHERE id AND user_id`); malformed JSON, bad kind/period/value (regex + range, also DB checks) → 400; lens ids checked against the shipped file; mutations carry the app's Origin in tests (AOS-618-ready) behind the AOS-615 body cap. Labels render as text (Playwright check). Nothing logged.
- **Data:** owner-chosen goals; not sensitive beyond analytics. No new dependency or listener.

## Proposed decision D-86 — Goals freeze their baseline; streaks are no-order days
2026-10-18 · Proposed (background agent) · Type: Product · Tasks: AOS-907 · Builds on D-46, D-80, D-82
- **Kinds:** cut a lens by X% (1..90) vs the 3 full months before the goal began, or keep it under ₹Y; weekly (Mon–Sun) or monthly, in the user's zone; one goal per lens.
- **Baseline frozen at creation** (per week = total × 7 ÷ days; per month = total ÷ 3); target rounded down to the rupee. A cut with nothing to cut from is refused.
- **Progress:** up to 12 periods since the start; refunds net off; met = spent ≤ target.
- **Streak:** consecutive days without an order in the lens ending today, and the best run in the last year since the first day with data; refunds don't break it.
- **Shown** on the leaks page and Home; goals don't push (limits do, D-83).
- **Rejected:** goals as flagged limits; a moving baseline.
