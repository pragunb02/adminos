## Description
The first Wealth screens, calm (D-49):
- `/wealth`: the total value of the owner's mutual funds, invested, gain (sign and words, not colour alone), return (absolute under a year, XIRR from a year), realised gains, "prices as of" with stale and valued-at-cost counts; holdings by asset class with folio (last 4), plan, value, invested, gain, return, price date; **"How we got this"** per holding (units, price and its source and date, value, FIFO cost incl. stamp duty, gain, the return's basis); **Needs review** (holdings reconciliation blocked, left out of the totals); money in and out of investments over 90 days (AOS-815) with unmatched bank payments.
- `/wealth/import`: upload a CAMS/KFintech "Detailed" CAS with its password; the result says what was added, what adds up and what needs review; the imports list with **Undo**.
- **Review tab:** an "Investments that don't add up" block (hidden when everything reconciles).
- API: `GET /api/v1/wealth/holdings` (lots × prices × returns, derived on read).

## Why
D-79 and S8.6: the owner can see their mutual funds end to end, with the working shown, before anything else is added.

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Where numbers are made:** (a) the browser combines holdings, prices and lots; (b) **one API call that returns finished, exact numbers and their working**. (b): one source of truth (D-45), no float maths in the browser, and the working is the server's own.
  2. **"How we got this":** (a) a fixed help text; (b) **the steps with this holding's own numbers**, generated by the API from the values it used. Chosen (b) (D-57/D-67 spirit: facts from code).
  3. **Blocked holdings:** (a) shown with a warning inside the totals; (b) **kept out of the totals and listed apart** on Wealth and in Review. Chosen (b) (D-79: blocked, shown in Review).
  4. **Placement:** under More now (no D-49 change); the owner decides later.
- **Decision:** proposed **D-134** below.
- **Impact:** a new route; web pages; MSW and Playwright mock defaults for the wealth endpoints.

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** `GET /api/v1/wealth/holdings` (read-only); pages `/wealth`, `/wealth/import`; the Review block. The upload goes to the existing `POST /wealth/imports/cas` (AOS-805: auth, CSRF, 20 MB, form shape).
- **Abuse and what stops it:**
  - *Signed out / forged / refused session:* the route is in `authenticate("auth-jwt")` (route test, route inventory); both pages redirect to login when signed out (PAGES) and end at login when the API refuses the session (Playwright).
  - *Another user's data:* every read is scoped by the token's user; IdorTest list row; route test.
  - *Untrusted text (T4, a crafted statement):* scheme/AMC names, reasons and issues are React text, never HTML (attack specs check no `<img>` and no script run).
  - *Identifiers:* only the masked folio (••••4/56) reaches the browser; the full folio never does (route test).
  - *The statement password:* sent once in the upload form, cleared from the field after the request, never put in the URL or storage (Playwright).
- **Data at rest:** nothing new stored. **Dependencies:** none.
