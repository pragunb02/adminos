## Description
Add a `ForecastComponent` that projects monthly bills (electricity, phone, broadband) which usually arrive later in the month but have not arrived yet, from the user's own history.

## Why
A follow-up from AOS-714 (month-end forecast and money left until salary, D-76): the month-end figure under-counts in the first half of the month when a regular bill is still to come (AOS-714 Risks).

## Scope
- **In:** a component per recurring biller, with its expected amount and day from past bills; shown as its own part.
- **Out:** bills with no history; guessing new billers.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A biller seen at least 3 months running and not yet billed this month is projected once, with its usual amount and day (`MonthlyBillProjectorTest`, `MonthEndForecasterTest`, `ForecastServiceTest`).
- [x] Once the bill arrives, the projection is replaced, never counted twice (`MonthlyBillProjectorTest` arrival cases; `ForecastServiceTest` › an arrived bill is counted once as a due).
- [x] The backtest (`forecastReport`) does not get worse; the baseline is never lowered (identical numbers, `baseline.json` untouched; new late-bills measurement: MAPE 10.91% → 9.67%).

## Security
D-100 threat review (ENGINEERING §4a).
- **What it exposes:** no new route, input, upload, outbound call, job, secret, dependency or listener. `GET /api/v1/forecast` (session only, per-user rate limit, unchanged) gains a `projected_bills` part whose items carry a biller name, an expected date, an amount, the bill type and how many months it was seen. No new stored data: projections are computed on read from the user's own `bills` rows.
- **Abuse and what stops it:**
  - **A stranger / a forged token:** the route is unchanged inside `authenticate("auth-jwt")`; the route-inventory test passes with no new entry.
  - **Another user's bills (IDOR):** bills are read with the session's user id only, and the service also drops any row of another user (`ForecastServiceTest` › another user's bills are never projected).
  - **A hostile bill email (the biller name is parsed from untrusted mail by the Go worker):** the name is carried as data, never interpreted; the web renders it as a text node (Vitest + Playwright: `<img src=x onerror=…>` shows as text, no element injected, the handler never runs). The unicode-aware grouping key can't crash on bidi or combining characters (unit tests).
  - **Inflating the forecast with fake bills:** an attacker who can get bill mail parsed could already add a known due; a projection needs 3 months running of the same biller and only ever adds the median of real amounts, once per month. Utility types only.
  - **Flooding:** at most 500 bills are read (`MAX_BILLS`); over that nothing is projected (the read is oldest-first, so a cut would drop this month's bills and could double one).
- **Values in logs:** nothing is logged; `PastBill` and `ProjectedBill` `toString` hold no amount or biller (tests).
- **Data at rest:** none new.
- **AI:** none; deterministic (D-67).
- **Secrets / fixtures:** synthetic names ("Sample Power Co", "Example Mobile") and amounts only.

## Resolution
- **Domain:** `forecast/ProjectedBills.kt` — `MonthlyBillProjector` (pure) learns each regular monthly biller and projects the bills not arrived yet; `ProjectedBillsComponent` counts them on their expected days inside a window (D-171):
  - only utility bills (electricity, mobile, internet, water, gas); rent and EMI are usually mandates too, insurance is yearly, credit-card and pay-later bills are transfers (D-39);
  - a biller (type + name with case/punctuation ignored, any script + account ref) billed in each of the 3 calendar months before a month, not yet billed in it, is projected at the median of those 3 amounts on the median due day (clamped to short months), only when that day is after today;
  - **arrived** (never counted twice) = any bill of the same type due in that month or within ±10 days of the expected day, whatever its name, account, amount or status; next month is projected only when this month's real bill is in;
  - a day that has passed without a bill isn't guessed (it may have been paid outside AdminOS).
- **Wiring:** `ForecastService` reads the user's bills from 6 months back through next month (cap 500, over it nothing is projected) and passes the projections in `ForecastInput.projectedBills`; `componentsFor` = due sources → projected bills → everyday. They feed month-end and money left until salary; the range moves with them but isn't widened (only the everyday part is uncertain).
- **API:** a `projected_bills` part in `monthEnd.parts` and `untilSalary.parts`; `DueDto` gains nullable `billType`, `monthsSeen`, `seenFrom`.
- **Web:** "Bills not arrived yet" as its own line and bar in "How we got this", each bill with its expected day, amount and "usual amount, billed N months running"; mock-API default includes it.
- **Measured:** the main backtest is identical (its dues are an oracle); a new late-bills measurement (the utility bill known only 7 days before its due date) improves MAPE from 10.91% to 9.67% (on the 10th: 14.98% → 12.51%), enforced by a test.
- **Tests:** `MonthlyBillProjectorTest` (38), `ProjectedBillsComponentTest` (5), new cases in `MonthEndForecasterTest`, `ForecastServiceTest`, `ForecastRoutesTest`, `ForecastPartsTest`, `ForecastBacktestTest`; Vitest `ForecastCard.test.tsx` (+4); Playwright `e2e/projected-bills.spec.ts` (3 × 3 projects).
- **Known limits (accepted, D-171):** a bill paid outside AdminOS before its usual day, with no bill row, is still projected once; one arrived bill of a type hides every other projection of that type that month (two homes' electricity) — the safe, under-counting side.
- **Found, not fixed:** PAY_LATER bills are counted as known dues by `ForecastService.toKnownDue` (only credit cards are dropped), which double-counts against D-89; `DuesService` never de-duplicates bills against mandates (an EMI bill and its e-NACH both count).
