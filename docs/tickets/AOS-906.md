## Description
Once a week: what was spent against the usual week (median of the last 8), the best and worst habit lens, how the limits went, and exactly one concrete suggestion backed by numbers, e.g. "Quick commerce: 9 orders, avg ₹410, 5 after 22:00. A ₹900 weekly limit would have saved ₹2,790." Owner's change (2026-10-17): the report card can say **"a suggested limit is ready"** (suggestions themselves never push, AOS-904).

## Why
D-80: one report a week that tells the owner what to change. BRD §5.6.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The week's spent vs the usual week (median of the 8 seven-day windows before, the same weekdays), or "not enough weeks yet" with fewer than 2 weeks of data.
- [x] Best (most below its usual) and worst (most above) lens; none without a usual.
- [x] Each limit's outcome on the week's last day: kept, or over by ₹X (weekly), or month to date (monthly).
- [x] "A suggested limit is ready on the leaks page." when the leaks page has a suggestion.
- [x] Exactly one suggestion, deterministic, every number from the facts.
- [x] It arrives inside the weekly briefing (one notification a week), with a structured copy.

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **A separate report-card service, job and notification:** a second weekly push and a second schedule, against D-80's "few nudges"; it would also duplicate the briefing's when/once-a-week logic (D-57).
  2. **Extend `WeeklyBriefingService` with pluggable sections** (chosen): the briefing owns *when* and *once per week*; a `BriefingSection` port (owned by the briefing, implemented by the coach) adds lines and a structured part. The briefing stays one notification; the coach doesn't depend on briefing internals, and the briefing doesn't depend on the coach (dependency inversion, wired by Koin `getAll`).
  3. **Rewrite `BriefingWriter` to call coach services directly:** couples the agent domain to the coach domain.
- **Decision:** option 2. **Patterns:** port + adapter (`BriefingSection` / `ReportCard`), Strategy list for suggestions (`SuggestionRule`, ordered: would-have-saved → late night → below usual → steady; the first that applies is the one suggestion; a new rule is one entry).
  - **"Sunday" report card:** it covers the briefing's 7 days. With the default briefing time (Monday 08:00) that is the Monday–Sunday week just ended, delivered the morning after Sunday. The owner can move the briefing day/time in Settings; the usual week is always the 8 windows with the same weekdays, so comparisons stay like for like.
  - **Would-have-saved cap:** the owner's accepted weekly limit on that lens if there is one, else the lens's usual − 10% rounded down to ₹50 (the D-83 rule), at least ₹100. Needs ≥ 3 orders.
  - **Usual week:** median of 8 weeks (weeks with no spending count as ₹0), only when ≥ 2 had spending.
- **Extensibility:** another weekly section is one `BriefingSection` class; another suggestion is one `SuggestionRule`.
- **Impact:** `agent/briefing/BriefingSection.kt` (new port), `WeeklyBriefingService` (optional `sections`, prompt version `weekly-v2`), Koin `getAll<BriefingSection>()`, `domain/coach/report/ReportCard.kt`. No schema change; no web change (the Briefings page already shows multi-line text).

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** no new route, input, table, job or dependency; the briefing text gains lines built from the owner's own numbers and labels.
- **Abuse:** nothing an attacker can send reaches it; labels are rendered as text by the existing Briefings page (React escaping) and Web Push shows plain text. Briefing scoping (per user) is unchanged.
- **Data / logs:** stored in the existing briefing row (structured copy has exact money strings, no raw text); nothing new logged.
- **Playwright:** no new screen or flow.

## Proposed decision D-85 — The report card is a section of the weekly briefing, with exactly one rule-based suggestion
2026-10-18 · Proposed (owner: the report card can say "a suggested limit is ready") · Type: Architecture / Product · Tasks: AOS-906 · Builds on D-57, D-67, D-80, D-83
- **Where:** a `BriefingSection` (port owned by the briefing, implemented by the coach) appends the report card to the weekly briefing; one notification a week; briefing format version `weekly-v2`.
- **What:** spent vs the usual week (median of the 8 same-weekday windows before; none with fewer than 2 weeks of data); best and worst lens vs their usual; limit outcomes on the week's last day; "A suggested limit is ready on the leaks page." when one exists; exactly one suggestion.
- **Suggestion rules, first that applies wins:** (1) would-have-saved: the biggest lens with ≥ 3 orders above its cap (accepted weekly limit, else usual − 10% down to ₹50, ≥ ₹100); (2) late night: ≥ 3 orders after the late-night start, with their total; (3) below usual by ≥ 10%: the saving and its monthly equivalent (× 4); (4) steady: the week's habit total (always applies).
- **"Sunday":** the briefing's 7 days; by default Mon–Sun, delivered Monday 08:00.
- **Rejected:** a separate weekly push (more nudges, duplicate scheduling); the briefing calling coach services directly (couples domains); AI-written suggestions (D-67, D-80).
