## Description
Money that leaves on its own: UPI AutoPay, e-NACH (loans and insurance) and card standing instructions. AdminOS now:
- learns each mandate from bank SMS;
- matches its debits;
- marks those transactions recurring;
- shows the mandates on Subscriptions;
- puts their debits on the dues calendar.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **SMS notices:** set-up, pre-debit, pause, resume and revoke messages are read for UPI AutoPay, e-NACH/NACH/ECS and card standing instructions. The details kept are payee, amount or limit, frequency, UMN/UMRN, the debit date (pre-debit), validity and bank. The text is never stored (D-33).
- [x] **Message precedence:**
  - OTPs and failed set-ups are still dropped first.
  - A pre-debit notice ("will be debited on …") is a mandate notice, not a dropped "upcoming payment".
  - A debit that has happened is still a transaction, marked `mandate` (and `umn`). Only those two markers travel into the transaction.
- [x] **Identity:** the UMN/UMRN, matched without case. Without one, the same payee (case, punctuation and company suffixes ignored) on the same rail; another rail is another mandate.
- [x] **Order and duplicates:**
  - the same message twice changes nothing;
  - an older message arriving late (SMS history) never undoes a newer status;
  - a pre-debit date only moves forward;
  - a pre-debit amount never replaces the mandate's own amount;
  - we can learn of a mandate from a pre-debit or a revoke alone.
- [x] **Debits:**
  - **Evidence:** the UMN in the debit SMS is enough; otherwise a similar payee name, an amount within the mandate's limit (5% slack), and either the mandate marker or a date within 3 days of the expected one.
  - **Ties:** two equal mandates match nothing.
  - **Excluded:** refunds and income never match.
  - **On a match:** the last debit is recorded, the next one moves on by frequency, a one-time mandate is completed, and the transaction is marked recurring with the mandate's ID, once.
- [x] **Today's view:**
  - past its validity a mandate shows as **expired**;
  - the next debit rolls forward by schedule;
  - active, periodic mandates with nothing taken for two cycles are **dormant** ("still needed? revoke it in your UPI app or bank");
  - active mandates come first, soonest debit first.
- [x] **`GET /api/v1/mandates`:** returns the status, amounts as strings, and whether a reference exists. The UMN itself is never returned.
- [x] **Dues calendar:** mandate debits show as "AutoPay" items, projected like renewals. A subscription paid by a mandate shows only once.
- [x] **Web:** Subscriptions has an "AutoPay & mandates" section, with loading, empty, error-with-retry, statuses and the dormant hint.

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Mandates as their own model, from SMS notices, matched to debits after commit** (chosen). This uses the AOS-505 hook.
  2. **Fold them into subscriptions.** Mandates aren't subscriptions: loans, utilities "as presented" and insurance don't fit, and their status comes from the bank.
  3. **Gmail mandate emails first.** Gmail isn't connected yet (AOS-401), and SMS is the primary source for the owner.
- **Decision:** option 1 (D-56). **Patterns:**
  - a parser `object` (same Strategy family as the bank parsers);
  - Observer (`MandateDebitListener`);
  - a pure matcher;
  - a view model computed for "today".
- **Extensibility:** a new rail or wording is one regex row. Gmail mandate emails (AOS-401) can produce the same `MandateNotice`.
- **Impact:**
  - `domain/financial/mandates/*`;
  - `MandateSmsParser` and `MandateDebitMarker`;
  - `SmsParserRegistry`: mandate notices come before the "upcoming payment" drop rule;
  - `SmsIntake`: a new `Mandate` result, plus a `mandates` count in the raw-SMS batch response;
  - `ToTransactionStep` carries the two markers;
  - `DuesService` (the MANDATE kind);
  - V22 `mandates`, with a unique (user, lower(UMN));
  - web: `Mandates`, the dues wording, types and fixtures.

## Verification
- **API:** 1,222 unit and 106 integration tests, 0 disabled, 99.3% lines.
  - **Parser:** each kind on synthetic HDFC/ICICI formats, the drop-rule order, the debit markers, and the date and payee edge cases.
  - **Service:** identity, idempotency, late messages, pre-debit forward-only, learning from a notice alone, and frequency.
  - **Debits:** UMN, the date stand-in, exclusions, ties, and one-time.
  - **View:** expired, rolling and dormant.
  - **Sorting and dues:** list order, and a subscription replaced by its mandate.
  - **Flow:** SMS → pipeline → listener → recurring transaction.
  - **Route:** the UMN is never shown.
  - **Postgres:** round trip, the case-insensitive unique UMN.
- **Web:** 318 unit tests, 98.5% lines / 96% branches; Playwright 232 passed + 7 deliberate skips.

## Risks / notes
- **Found by the tests and fixed before merge:**
  - the default (SMART) date parsing turned "31/02/2026" into 28 Feb, so dates are now parsed STRICT;
  - "mandate for Rs 100 …" was taken as payee "for Rs 100 created", so payee stop-words are now rejected;
  - the trailing "." on a UMN at the end of a sentence was kept.
- **Synthetic samples:** the SMS samples are approximations of common formats. Bank templates vary, so check a few real messages (AOS-211).
- **No new reminders:** mandate debits don't send their own reminders, because banks already send a pre-debit SMS. They appear on the dues calendar.
- **Read-only:** AdminOS can't revoke a mandate (that happens in the UPI app or the bank). It says so.
