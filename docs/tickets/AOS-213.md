## Description
"Rs 50 cashback will be credited…" was read as a real credit of Rs 50. Only "will be debited" was dropped. Promised
and future credits ("will be credited", "shall be credited", "to be credited", "credited within N days",
"eligible for cashback", "refund … initiated") must not become transactions, and real credits must still be read.

## Why
A promised credit counted as income inflates the month's inflow and hides spending (AOS-S2.5: numbers I can trust).
Many such promises never arrive, or arrive later as their own "credited" SMS, which would then count twice.

## Scope
- **In:** SMS and WhatsApp bank messages (both go through `SmsParserRegistry`).
- **Out:** Gmail (Go worker, its own rules); upcoming debits (already dropped).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Promised credits in HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak and generic formats are ignored with reason `promised credit, not a transaction`
- [x] A real debit or credit in the same SMS as a promise is still read, with its own amount, not the promised one
- [x] Credits that already happened ("cashback credited to your A/c", "refund … has been credited") stay credits
- [x] Upcoming debits are still dropped as before

## Design
- **Context:** drop rules are whole-message regexes in `SmsParserRegistry`. Dropping the whole message on "will be
  credited" would lose real debits such as "Rs 2,000 spent at X. Cashback of Rs 100 will be credited".
- **Options considered:** 1) one more whole-message drop regex (loses the real debit beside a promise);
  2) remove only the clause that promises, then parse what's left; if nothing transactional is left, it was a promise.
- **Decision:** option 2 (`PromisedCredits.strip`). Clauses end at `;`, `!`, a new line, " and ", or a full stop
  before a capital letter that isn't an abbreviation ("Rs.", "A/c no.", "Dr."). The rest of the text is kept exactly
  as written. **Patterns:** Specification (one phrase list), shared by every bank parser.
- **Extensibility:** a new promise phrase is one entry in `PROMISE`.
- **Impact:** domain only (`domain/ingestion/sms`). No schema, route or contract change.

**D-120** (recorded in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md)): a promised or future credit is not a transaction; only
the clause that promises is removed, so a real debit or credit in the same message is still read, without the
promised amount. Rejected: dropping the whole message (loses real debits beside a cashback promise).

## Security
- **Exposes:** nothing new (no route, input, storage or dependency). Same 2,000-character SMS limit.
- **Abuse:** a hostile SMS could try to hide a real debit behind a promise phrase, or make a regex slow.
  - Hiding: only the promising clause is removed; a debit in another clause is still read. A debit written inside the
    promising clause itself is dropped, which loses one transaction but never invents money (fails safe).
  - Slowness: all quantifiers are bounded or linear; a unit test runs 2,000-character repetitive hostile text in bounded time.
- Reasons never quote the text (D-33). Fixtures are synthetic.

## Risks / notes
- A promise written in one clause together with a real credit ("Rs 500 credited, Rs 50 cashback will be credited" with
  a comma only) would drop both; commas are not clause ends because they appear inside amounts and payee names.
