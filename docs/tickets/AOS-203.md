## Parts
1. **Parsers** (transport-independent): `SmsParserRegistry` with per-bank `BankSmsParser` strategies, drop rules, `SmsMessage` → `IngestEvent`.
2. **Device token:** a scoped (`ingest:sms`), revocable token via the existing JWT + session system (approved by Pragun).
3. **Transport (D-35):** Mac Messages reader (Text Message Forwarding + host-side Go reader of `chat.db`), posting to `/ingest/sms/raw` on localhost.
4. **Setup guide** for the chosen transport.

## Transport research (2026-10-16)
- **Tailscale `serve`:** private tailnet, HTTPS; the Shortcut works over the iOS VPN. SMS are lost while the Mac sleeps.
- **Cloudflare Tunnel + Access:** service-token headers work from Shortcuts. Cloudflare terminates TLS (sees plaintext); needs a domain; public hostname gated at the edge.
- **ngrok / Funnel / localhost.run:** public, a third party sees traffic, weak access control. Rejected.
- **LAN bind:** exposes the app to the whole Wi-Fi, no TLS. Rejected.
- **Mac Messages reader:** zero exposure; catches up after sleep; needs Full Disk Access; on Ventura+ the text sits in `attributedBody`. **Chosen for now.**
- **iCloud Drive/Notes relay:** not end-to-end encrypted without ADP; sync timing unpredictable. Rejected.
