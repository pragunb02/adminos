## Description
One push each evening at a time the owner chooses (default **21:30** in their zone): what was spent today, the top leaks of the day, and where the limits stand. Optionally silent on a day with no spending. Delivered through the dispatcher's channels (D-51), at most once per day.

## Why
D-80: the coach reaches the owner without them opening the app; one evening digest a day is the agreed rhythm.

## Scope
- **In:** `EveningDigestService` (facts → deterministic text → one `COACH` notification), a per-day ledger, a polling job, settings (on/off, time, skip zero days) in `coach_settings`, Settings UI.
- **Out:** the weekly report card (AOS-906).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] At or after the chosen local time, one digest is sent for today; never two for the same day, across restarts and instances; a missed evening is not sent the next day.
- [x] It says today's spent (the console's number, D-45/D-46), up to two top leaks with orders and late-night orders, and up to two limits nearest their amount; every number is a fact from analytics, leaks or limits.
- [x] "Skip on a zero day" sends nothing when nothing was spent; without it, a zero day says so.
- [x] Off means no digest; the time and zone are the user's (DST-safe); defaults 21:30, on, don't skip.
- [x] Settings shows and changes the switch, the time and the skip option; bad times are 400.

## Design
- **Options considered:**
  1. **`Schedule.Daily(21:30, zone)` job:** one slot per day, but the time and zone are per user and editable, and a changed time would need a new schedule.
  2. **Poll every 15 minutes; the service decides per user; a ledger row per (user, day) is the claim** (chosen; the same shape as the weekly briefing, AOS-506). Claim then send: at most once.
  3. **Store digests as `briefings` with a daily type:** reuses a table, but fills the Briefings page with daily rows and couples two features.
- **Decision:** option 2. **Patterns:** a pure `DigestWriter` over `DigestFacts`; facts gathered from `AnalyticsService` (today's spent), `LeakService` (top lenses today) and `LimitService` (statuses).
  - **Window:** due from the chosen time until local midnight; nothing for a day that has passed.
  - **Skip on a zero day:** when skipping, nothing is claimed, so a payment later that evening still gets its digest at the next poll.
  - **Quiet hours** (default from 22:00) apply like any non-critical push: a 22:30 digest time is delivered when quiet hours end; Settings says so.
- **Extensibility:** a new digest line is one fact and one writer line; a morning digest would be a second `kind` in the same ledger.
- **Impact:** `domain/coach/digest/*`, `coach_settings` gains three columns, `coach_digests_sent` table (`V36__coach_digest.sql`, was tentative V42), Settings UI.

## Security (D-100, docs/ENGINEERING.md §4a)
- **Exposes:** three new fields on the authenticated `GET/PATCH /coach/settings`; a 15-minute job; one table. No new routes.
- **Abuse and what stops it:** settings are keyed by the token's user (no ids); signed out → 401 for GET and PATCH (route test); `digestTime` must be `HH:mm` (00:00–23:59) → 400 otherwise, tested with injection text; PATCH is a cookie mutation under the app-wide `OriginCheck` (AOS-618, D-94) and its tests send the app's Origin; the AOS-615 body cap applies. Spam: at most one digest per (user, day) by primary key, race-tested on Postgres. The text only contains the owner's own numbers and lens/limit labels; logs carry the user id and the date only.
- **Data at rest / listeners / dependencies:** settings and sent-dates only; nothing sensitive; none new.
