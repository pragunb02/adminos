## Description
The owner travels often between two cities. A trip's real cost is the tickets **plus** the cabs and food around them, and the owner wants to see it as one total.

This task groups:
- travel bookings: flights (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa), trains (IRCTC), buses (redBus) and the travel agencies (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Cleartrip), read from transactions, booking emails and IRCTC SMS;
- the spends around them.

The result is trips the owner can rename, add to, remove from or dismiss. There is an API, a web Trips view (list and detail) with a "trips this year" total, and a deterministic rule measured on a synthetic eval set with a committed baseline.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **Bookings** come from three sources:
  - transactions to travel brands;
  - booking **emails** (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa, IRCTC, redBus; per-sender parsers; synthetic fixtures);
  - **IRCTC SMS**.
  An email or SMS gives the **journey date**. It is tied to its payment with the AOS-903 `PaymentMatcher` (exact amount, booking time, the brand as payee; auto-links only). (`bookings_test.go`, `TripServiceTest`, `TripGrouperTest`)
- [x] **A pure, deterministic rule:**
  - each booking is a leg on its journey date (else its booking date);
  - legs within **7 days** of the previous one chain into one trip;
  - the trip spans the first to the last leg, and a single leg spans its day and the next;
  - spends in the span in the transport or food categories join the trip;
  - a booking refunded in full or cancelled is not a leg;
  - dates are the user's own.
  (`TripGrouperTest`)
- [x] **Totals:** spends minus their linked refunds (D-40). "Trips this year" sums the year's trips. (`TripServiceTest`, `TripRoutesTest`)
- [x] **Edits:**
  - rename a trip;
  - add a transaction (it then belongs to that trip only);
  - remove one (never auto-added again);
  - dismiss a trip.
  All of these survive regrouping (the trip ID comes from its first leg). Another user's trip or transaction is 404. (`TripGrouperTest`, `TripServiceTest`, `TripRoutesTest`)
- [x] **API:**

## Design
- **Context:**
  - Bookings already show up as transactions (merchant-catalogue travel brands).
  - The journey date lives only in booking emails and SMS: a return ticket booked weeks ahead otherwise lands on its booking day.
  - Refunds are linked by the pipeline (D-40).
  - `PaymentMatcher` (D-88) ties a document to its payment.
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Compute trips on read** with a pure grouper, storing only the user's edits (`trip_edits`, keyed by a stable trip ID from the first leg) — **chosen.**
     - Trips always agree with the latest transactions, refunds and categories.
     - The rule is measurable.
     - The edits are small.
  2. **Materialise trips** with a nightly job.
     - Stale between runs.
     - Reconciling a regrouping with stored members is error-prone.
  3. **Manual trips only:** not what the owner asked for.
- **The chain gap is measured, not guessed:**
  - On the eval set, 10 days merged a one-way trip with another trip 9 days later (pairwise precision 0.82).
  - 7 days keeps them apart (0.98) and still joins a 6-day trip home.
  - A longer trip home without a booking email for its return splits in two, which is visible and fixable with an edit. `TripRules` holds the gap and the "around" categories as data.
- **Patterns:**
  - Strategy and Registry (Go booking parsers per sender; the IRCTC SMS parser in the registry);
  - a pure `TripGrouper`;
  - Repository (`TravelBookingRepository`, `TripEditRepository`: in-memory and Exposed);
  - `PaymentMatcher` reuse.
- **Impact:**
  - DB: `V52__trips.sql` (`travel_bookings`, `trip_edits`).
  - Go:
    - `internal/bookings` (new; it reuses the receipts label engine, now exported);
    - Gmail filters (booking senders, booking subjects only);
    - the handler routes booking senders, and `persistBookings` upserts (confirmation and cancellation merge).
  - API:
    - `domain/financial/trips` (models, grouper, service, routes, SMS bookings, in-memory repositories);
    - `TravelBookingSmsParser`, the registry, `SmsIntake` (`Booking` result), `RawSmsRoutes`, the WhatsApp import;
    - Exposed repositories and tables;
    - Koin; `FinancialModule`.
  - Web:
    - `/trips`, `/trips/[id]`;
    - `lib/trips.ts`, `lib/types.ts`;
    - the More link;
    - e2e mocks and the navigation list.

## Eval (synthetic, committed baseline)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Assignment precision | **0.9688** |
| Assignment recall | **0.8857** |
| Pairwise precision | **0.9839** |
| Pairwise recall | **0.8714** |

**Known misses, kept in the set on purpose:**
- an agency booking with no booking email lands on its booking day (scenario E);
- a one-way flight trip only spans its day and the next, so the last cab of a longer stay is missed (G2).

The baseline is raised only deliberately, with the change that moved it.

## Security (D-100, ENGINEERING §4a)
- **What is exposed:**
  - 7 authenticated routes (4 mutations);
  - new input: booking emails from 6 domains and IRCTC SMS;
  - new stored data: travel bookings (brand, mode, journey date, amount, a PNR hash) and trip edits (name, IDs);
  - two web screens.
  - No new dependency, listener or outbound call.

| Threat | What stops it |
|---|---|
| **A forged booking email** (it could move a trip or invent a leg) | The parser is chosen by the address domain only (display name ignored; look-alike, subdomain-of-another and two-@ addresses get none). Gmail's SPF/DKIM/DMARC apply first. A booking is never money. It ties to a payment only on an exact amount, the time window and the brand as payee. A leg without a payment adds no money. The user can dismiss a trip. |
| **Hostile email content** | Bodies are capped (64 KB, 2,000 lines). Patterns are RE2. Look-alike digits are not dates. A journey must fall between 1 day before and 400 days after the booking. Amounts are capped at ₹5 lakh. The PNR is letters and digits only, and stored as SHA-256 only. A 100,000-line `<script>` body is refused quickly. (`bookings_test.go`) |
| **Hostile SMS** | Only IRCTC-shaped texts with a 10-digit PNR and a valid DOJ are read. Bank alerts that mention IRCTC are still bank alerts. Texts over 1,000 characters are refused, and implausible fares are dropped. OTP, promotional and personal-number senders are refused by the registry. (`TripServiceTest`) |
| **Another user's IDs** | Trips are computed from the signed-in user's data only. Unknown or other users' trip and transaction IDs are 404 (`TRIP_001`/`TRIP_002`). |
| **Bad input** | The year must be within 5 years. Names are trimmed, 1–80 characters, with no control characters. IDs must be UUIDs. Malformed JSON is 400. Bodies over 1 MiB are 413 (D-92). |
| **Cross-site mutations** | The session cookie is SameSite=Strict. The tests send the app's `Origin`. There is no separate Origin check in the API (gap noted in AOS-903). |
| **Untrusted text in the web** | Trip names and merchants are React text. Unit and e2e tests check that `<img onerror>` stays text. A malformed `year` in the URL is ignored. |
| **Data at rest** | Bookings are travel facts already implied by the transactions. The PNR is hashed. No names, seats or stations are kept. |

## Proposed decision D-90
### D-90 — Trips are computed on read by a measured rule; only edits are stored
2026-10-18 · Proposed (AOS-910, background agent) · Type: Architecture / Product · Tasks: AOS-910, AOS-901 · Builds on D-40, D-61, D-67, D-88
- **Legs:** each travel booking is a leg on its journey date.
  - Bookings come from transactions to travel brands, booking emails (per-sender parsers in the worker) and IRCTC SMS.
  - The journey date comes from the email or SMS tied to the payment by `PaymentMatcher` (auto only); otherwise it is the booking date.
  - A booking refunded in full or cancelled is not a leg.
- **Trips:**
  - legs within 7 days chain into one trip;
  - the trip spans the first to the last leg (a single leg spans its day and the next);
  - transport and food spends in the span join it;
  - days are the user's own.
  The parameters are data (`TripRules`).
- **Total** = members − their linked refunds.
- **Edits are the only stored state** (`trip_edits`, keyed by an ID derived from the first leg): name, added (that trip only), removed (never auto-added again), dismissed.
- **Measured** like D-61: a synthetic eval set reports assignment and pairwise precision and recall. The baseline is committed (0.9688 / 0.8857 / 0.9839 / 0.8714), and a test fails below it.
- **Privacy:** bookings keep brand, mode, journey date, amount and a SHA-256 of the PNR only.
- **Rejected:**
  - materialised trips with a nightly job (stale, and hard to reconcile);
  - a 10-day chain gap (merged separate trips on the eval);
  - manual-only trips.
